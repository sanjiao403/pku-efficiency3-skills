## 1 Study

We measured interferon-beta release from three macrophage genotypes after
cytosolic DNA stimulation. Cells were stimulated at t = 0 and supernatant was
collected at 2, 6, 12 and 24 h. Each condition had four biological replicates;
values in measurements.csv are group means in pg/mL with the standard deviation in
the adjacent column.

## 2 Findings

Wild type macrophages released a peak of 320 pg/mL IFN-beta at 6 h. DHHC3
knockdown cells released 612 pg/mL at the same time point, 1.9 fold higher than
wild type. DHHC3 catalytic-dead knock-in (DHHC3 CD) behaved like wild type,
confirming that the effect requires catalytic activity rather than protein
abundance. By 24 h all genotypes converged to basal release below 40 pg/mL.
